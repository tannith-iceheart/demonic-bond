Hello, I'm not really sure how this is going to end up but I am Equinox and I am here to talk about my journey into witchcraft. This is going to be a personal video journal where I just talk about what I am learning about. I am not here to make guides about anything, I *will* be wrong about many things, feel free to correct me and learn along with me :)

The background footage in the video is just to give something to watch, any time I put up a picture I describe it in detail.

---

To start this off I guess I should start with my past experiences with it and what I expected from getting into this-

I grew up in a cult-like environment. And I don't mean people who practiced witchcraft in a coven or group, but people who used witchcraft as an excuse to make money off of victims and do horrible things. I knew the *idea* that witches existed, and grew up with a generally positive association with them, but I never really knew what that meant.

I am not going to talk about that cult today, that might be a later video but it's just too much to get into here. For the short of it though I had little exposure to any actual rituals *if* they did practice them.

My mother was the person to get me into this environment. My parents were divorced since I was 3 so I had only ever known just living with her and my brothers. She told me that I was some special thing that could sense spirits and walk other worlds, she told me I could do things that others couldn't

A lot of the things she told me, I assume, were not real beliefs of hers. just things that would keep me in control. It was less spell work and protection and more walking down a hiking trail and "feeling" that we were changing worlds, and I had to "act" like I was supposed to be in this world to not be separated from her. A lot of the things she said about it was very claim based, like she said she was / did things that she didn't.

---

Then the Witchtok happened. This was, very interesting. It started popping off right about when I was 16. It gave me the idea to start branching off my own practice instead of following whatever, thought based in-actionable thing my mom was doing? I started "researching" different things but it was unfortunately through tiktok.

At this point in my life I still had horrible social anxiety. I hated anything to do with other people, social media, or forums. It was just easy to use tiktok and I gave myself the excuse of it. I mean, I was also 16. I made a lot of stupid decisions when I was 16.

---

At this point my "practice" was pyromania, a tarot deck that I didn't understand, summoning things that I shouldn't, and dream work. Dream work is about the only thing I still feel confident in, and still am making a goal for my current practice. (that doesn't mean I wont have to unlearn stuff about that later though)

Sometime later, I met someone who didn't approve of witchcraft and fell out of it for a few years. Recently they suggested that I pick it up again. I am not going to get in why, but it wasn't suggested to me seriously. It was suggested and agreed upon under the impression that it was for ascetics and mental health trickery. - Suggested, for it to be what it used to be. 

---

That said- a lot of how I was introduced to witchcraft lead to how I expected this time to go. I expected to head into this just as foolish and fake it used to be. Not that I didn't believe in what I was doing just that.. I never really put intention or time into anything.

But it.. didn't.

I started looking for resources and felt pressed to actually learn this time. I have been improving on myself genuinely recently and I think that's a big factor in this change.

I actually started reading again because of this desire, this feeling that this is right for me. I haven't read in several years despite wanting to. I am doing physical crafts again, wanting to go outside again. This has genuinely felt.. different.

---

The first thing that I did was start reading an actual book about witchcraft, the first one that called to me just to get into the idea of it. It's really helped solidify my idea about it and my desire to continue down this path.

I know that I am easy to persuade, so I made a few different rules for myself while I studied practicing.

1- I must look for several different peoples, groups, and ideas. Even if something doesn't particularly appeal to me or sound fun, it should still be fun to at least learn about it.
2- I will avoid to watch, click on, believe anything that is vague. ie-  "this will find you when" "if you are x do this" "if you find this on this date do x"
3- Avoid absolutes. while learning I want to try and be as open minded as possible to learn how to do things the ethical way, and the way that works for me.
4- Even if I wont share it, write down as much as possible. It can be organized and fussed with later, but keeping tabs on what I'm doing is important.

---

The first thing that I wanted to research was cleansing and protection. I made a night time meditation for myself to cleanse myself of energies before sleeping so that I could start practicing lucid dreaming again.

The lucid dreaming was not intended to be spiritual, just practice for when I eventually did use it. At the time I started again it was difficult and took time but still possible. I dislike changing the dream if its something I can handle, even if its a nightmare, so even if I am in control of my in dream actions, I don't typically change the dream itself.

Then I had a extremely odd dream. This was still at a point where I was questioning if this kind of thing was good to me, if I should continue witchcraft. But it was the start of confirmation for me.

Even though I said I worked with spirits and guides as a kid, I am not really sure I did. I don't know if they are or ever were real. They never really felt like entities, more like spiritual golems I made to put on a show. So- before ever starting to worship or work with any entity I wanted to get my ground work in, I wanted to avoid anything heavy or advanced. And then I had this dream.

---

This dream contained a entity that signed their name as Sturmfur. I have no idea who this is, why they are here, or what they intend for me. However, as far as I can tell, their presence feels helpful and safe. 

In the dream, after I broke into lucid dreaming, they took me on a walk through old memories. The memories were placed on pedestals, they were artwork that was made for me, about me. It was very surreal. They warped from how I saw them then, to how I saw them now.

It felt like both an acknowledgement that I had come a long way, and also a warning that my journey isn't done. There is still more to improve.

---

After this dream I had a few other moments that made me feel like this was the correct path for me, but still had a feeling of fear and doubt about it. I still had the feeling I could be falling for some scam again, the fear that I would end up in the same place I was when I was a child.

I logically know that isn't true, and that is preventable if I watch it. But the fear still exists.

After that dream I continued reading and researching. The first thing that I wanted to do was clean out my old protection jar and make one that actually had intent in it. I wanted to make something. I didn't quite have all the materials I wanted though so I kept scrolling.

---

And then I saw a video on making wands. Now I still am not sure how one is supposed to use a wand. I'm sure for the practiced that probably sounds silly, but genuinely, I have absolutely no idea. I was never really interested in them, I never really felt a draw to it.

In fact- I felt uncomfortable. Like a weird semi-embarrassment about having one, to be clear I never had judgement of others, just myself about some really weird niche things. And so, because I am working on myself and being less judgmental of myself I decided to make one. I decided that I had the materials, and if I didn't use it, it was carving practice. It couldn't hurt to make one.

just an exercise in exploring the whys of my mind. no intention to use it, no intention for it to be connected with witchcraft.

I didn't really think much about it beforehand, I like red and dragons, so it would probably have red and black in it. Other than that I didn't really have any intent for it.

I went on a walk and found a cool stick, started carving with this old dull knife that made it hell but it's what I had on me. About 3 hours into carving, I just had the feeling that it should be themed after Sturmfur, and the dream they made me.

It's a very confusing feeling, because It wasn't *my* intention. I felt that it would be best used as that, I felt a push for it, but there was no solid idea of 'oh, I could theme it like this!'. It just, felt, right.

This is the most bizarre feeling in the world. I have alters, I *know* how they sound in my head. It wasn't a thought from one of them, it was a *intention* from *someone else*. I have never felt this before, and it's probably one of the main things keeping my fear from getting to me right now.

---

After I had carved out what I wanted of it, I painted it a dark blue that fades up into swirls of teals. I had this old wooden flower pin thing lying around and put it on the end, painting it too to match the "dream paint" that I was mimicking.

The paint faded up into to the wood, I left some of it exposed. I wrapped the end with some old dark green threaded wire I found the same day. I didn't realize it at the time but the color and pattern directly related to some of the things in the dream with Sturmfur.

I never thought out these actions, like I didn't decide that teal would look good. I didn't decide to find green vines to wrap it. I just felt it. I know I keep repeating that but its just so interesting to me. I have never felt like this before.

---

So anyways I now have a wand and a guide that I didn't intend to work with. I am super happy about both of them and super exited to continue on my journey. Since making the wand I have asked for a little more about Sturmfur and he seems to align with a lot of the same morals I have.

I think one of the bigger things about me feeling like this is, I don't want to say real because it was real to me before, but feeling a kind of *true* this time. is *because* its not what I expected or wanted out of this. I do genuinely think that's good though.

I'm outside of my comfort zone here, I'm learning new things and finding new passion. I am still so unsure about everything in this amazing massive universe, but I am grateful for the experiences I have had sense starting to practice again.

I am super exited to look into more things to do next, and to keep recording these things and journaling them. I found a lack of new experiences out there on witchcraft and I want to show that perspective. Even if I am absolutely terrified to post this, I promise I stared at the finished video for hours before being able to hit post.

I hope this can help someone, know that you aren't alone if you are also confused. And if you are practiced I hope that it can bring a new or familiar perspective. Please feel free to leave me advice too, its good to have more things to learn about. 

I am so exited to grow more here, and so happy I am where I am in life. Thank you for watching this video, It genuinely means a lot to me. I hope that your days are well and your sleep is what you desire.
