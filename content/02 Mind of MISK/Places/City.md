The city is absolutely massive, and honestly a lot like the jungle in function. The alters that don't seem to have set homes seem to show up here most often.

The deeper traveled into the city the darker it gets / more night time it gets. Red, Azule, and Pen live in the "always night" section, however Pen has a house in both the night and the part that changes time of day.

Its assumed that the maroon alter lives in the day section, however it hasn't been confirmed, just that she can manipulate that section.

Its incredibly crammed all the way through, with very little open space. There are lots and lots of levels, subway stations, and manufacturing like buildings, however there doesn't seem to be a lot of non-alter interaction. (only one small building has been seen with workers, and there isn't really ever any traffic)
