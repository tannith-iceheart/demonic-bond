The desert is a very very large area with not a lot in it. It's very open ended and allows for travel from any part of Misk. The initial idea for creation was a large space to hold information, and allow other alters to know / interact with that information

Dune is both the only ruler and creator of the desert location. In the far back corner Is his castle / palace thing, which also functions as a village. The palace has lots of rooms and information, some about where alters live, some about what they do, most of the information is in some way related to the inner world in one way or another

Unfortunately only two alters have access to the whole palace, Silver and Dune agreed that not all information should be accessible by all alters. This means that to get through the palace one usually needs a guide.

The rest of the dessert is quite sparse, mostly just rocky packed sand roads to get to the back corner. There is a cave entrance somewhere north-ish but it hasn't proven to have a lot of meaning.
