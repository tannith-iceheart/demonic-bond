The Center Castle is probably the most detailed part of misk. It is a massive city sized building that has multiple entrances. Some of those are open to enter, some are locked by key, and others locked by force (Silver would have to open them)

Inside the castle is a maze, halls and halls of very old styled furniture. The castle is always clean and taken care of, however the only inhabitant is Silver. Some of the hallways end in a dead end, some of them lead to locked doors, some of them are just empty of exits for miles.

Navigating the castle is something an alter must get permission for, though any alter can enter the castle, most cant navigate it and would need Silver to give them a door. There have been attempts to navigate it without help and its really just a nightmare.

Dune has a few keys for a portion of the building closest to the desert, and can navigate that section ok enough but still gets lost from time to time. Dune, maroon, Akumi, Red, and one other alter that doesn't have descriptors have been inside the castle multiple times.

The castle is the closest section to the "front room". Though all sections have multiple doors that lead there (except the jungle) the castle is "physically closest".
