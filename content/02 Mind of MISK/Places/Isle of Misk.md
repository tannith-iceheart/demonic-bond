Misk is still being explored, and it is very difficult to get information from the far reaches of misk to the waking world. The names of places, people who inhabit them, and how many places there are will change as new information is found.

Overall though, the main island is a circle separated into "slices" of territories. the north-east corner is a large patch of [[Desert]], under that an massive [[City]]. What is directly north is currently unknown, north-west (mostly west) is a large [[Jungle]]. There are caves somewhere, but its unsure if that is under all of misk or just a section of it. directly south is also a mystery. South west is an [[Ocean]].

In the center of the island is a large mountain that flattens out, on that a very very massive [[Center Castle|castle]] about the size of the mountain itself. The castle has portal-like access points from all over misk, and each one goes to different sections of the castle.

[[Trynk]] has a [[Pocket Dimension]] they carry on them, its access point is on Trynk themselves and doesn't connect to any one part of Misk.

[[The front room]] is weird because we can tell its "above" the island and even though we travel to and from it, there is no logical doorway or path to it. It seems to be accessible from anywhere on the island, some places more than others though.

There are two kinds of inhabitants in Misk, Rulers and Members.

Rulers rule over one of the "slices" of Misk, they can change and adapt that territory to what best fits their purposes and preferences. They can also create "npc" type objects for their territories (eg; city denizens, fish, fauna, etc.).

Members of misk live in these territories but can only change a very very small segment of it, usually only a room or maybe small neighborhood. Members can also make npc type objects but usually only one or two. (eg; roommate, battle rival, pets, etc.).

[[Silver]] has access to all of misk and though they don't, can change any of it.
