There isn't much to say about the ocean. It has two major sections shallow and deep.

The shallow section has a few small islands but is mostly made up of coral reefs. There aren't any alters that live in this section.

The deep end of the ocean is where Mesmur lives, There are plenty of non-alter things around in both the shallow and deep parts of the ocean.

Mesmur sleeps in a small cave at the bottom of the deep, its next to an underwater volcano so its warm. The deep has a lot of different caves.
