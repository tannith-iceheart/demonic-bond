The jungle is one of the first sections that was "discovered" or made in Misk. Unfortunately it was also one of the first sections forgotten.

The function of the jungle is a form of dissociation, alters go there to hide or 'disappear' from the outside world. There are a few sections within the jungle as well but it isn't easy to find them.

From what can be gathered most of the "retired" alters are there now. The current list of inhabitants may or may not be alters, its unsure weather the ones we are considering are non-alter characters made by Akumi, or if they were actually present in early childhood.

The main section of the jungle is a large temple. The area around it is very draconic or magical. There are a lot of non-alters in the area because its made as an escape.
