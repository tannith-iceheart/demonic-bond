### Front Pull

Angel is one of the main hosts, so they are usually in front room. If they aren't [[Red]] can call them to front. They enjoy being at front when doing tasks that take story building (comic, writing, or video planning)

### Role Specifics

Host / Co-host. Angel shares the host role with [[Red]], they switch out when the other gets tired or overly emotional. Angel holds more responsibility with relationships and deescalating upset situations.

### Appearances

Fallen: The form most often taken by Angel. Large black wings with white stripes (similar to that of a magpie). Long white hair that goes down to mid/lower back. Angel wears various gothic looking hoodies and long pants. Their appearance can be described as "fluffy and tired". Their eyes are bright greyish blue (the same color they use to identify themselves.)

Broken: The fallen form but with more scars. Their left leg is machine, and a long scar over their left eye. This form is more upset and sharp feeling, its taken when something incredibly emotional is happening

Machine: A full machine body, with accents of their identity color. this drone form has 4 arms that split into different tools. The origin of this is written down in [[01-24-26 ♦ Journal - 'A Second Life'|"A Second Life"]], a journal entry from Angel themselves.
