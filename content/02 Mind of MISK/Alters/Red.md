### Trigger Info

Red is the co-host (or host, depending on the day) so to call them to front all it takes is them being mentioned in conversation. Otherwise they will take over when they want to experience something, Crow calls on them, or a situation starts feeling unsafe.

---
### Appearances

Red has two forms, a "physical" form that can touch and manipulate things in Misk and a "ghost" form that's harder to control and is more of an emotional state. Both forms take on a more punk look, with short vibrant red hair. His forms are thin and fast, and a bit small compared to most of the other alters. He has a tattoo sleeve on his left arm and some other singular tattoos in other places, as well as a piercing on the upper bridge of their nose.

His Poltergeist form is violent looking, clothes becoming torn up and glowing eyes. Its not something that they like to think about because its described as a "uncontrollable rage", however its a very large part of them, and the form they fractured from.

The form they take most is a "vampiric" form. The differences this form adds is mostly just the sharpened teeth. This form can also appear as something more "demonic" with horns, bat like wings, and a long thin tail.

---
### Home in Misk

Red is a member of the [[City|cities]] night section. He has a small apartment several floors above ground level.

His apartment is incredibly messy, he collects things from all over Misk and kind of just throws them around his room. He has two cats, a black one and a calico. 

Red likes to leave the windows open, and when leaving the apartment takes the balcony rather than the door. The apartment is just as cold as the city.
