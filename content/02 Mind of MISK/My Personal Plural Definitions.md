How I mean something here, may differ from how someone else means it, and that's ok! Everyone's experience is different, this is just how I share mine <3

## Glossary
### A
**Admin**
	There is only one "admin", we aren't really sure why but they can modify and see *all* of MISK. This term is made for [[Silver]]'s page

**Age**
	The age the alter feels, or appears in headspace, this is sometimes a range that fluctuates.

**AKA**
	The name or nicknames the alter goes by, most alters have at least two names (color and chosen name)
### C
**Color**
	My head works really weird, I don't see in images or text (I can but it usually has to be prompted). I think in "colors" or emotions. 
		The best way I can relate it to is there is just some *vibe* that I can read, and that usually has a color attached to it. Because of this, before the form or name of an alter is visible, color is the easiest thing to read.
	Some alters don't have chosen names yet so they default to their "color"

**Console**
	The "desk" that is used to interact as the body, this is purely metaphorical, there is rarely ever an actual console in front room, but it helps us explain who is "in control" or has influence on an experience or action
### F
**Front Pull**
	Types of things, actions, desires or causes that encourages or forces an alter to [[front room]], or to console. 
### L
**Location**
	 Where the alter "lives" in headspace, a living place is an area an alter can modify in headspace. The amount they can "edit" a space depends on their authority (see: Ruler / Member / Admin)
### M
**Member**
	A member of MISK is an alter who isn't a ruler. They have a smaller section they have control over (eg, a room, house, temple, or cave)
### R
**Roles**
	 Roles are the main jobs that an alter does, or the "purpose" of their split, they are used in more of a "tagging" sense than a static title. See bottom of page for specific alter roles.

**Ruler**
	 An alter that oversees an entire section of MISK. They can modify and easily traverse these areas, and "members" can live within these areas.
	 Known sections of misk are: [[Center Castle]], [[City]], [[Desert]], [[Jungle]], [[Ocean]], [[Pocket Dimension]]
### S
**Species**
	 The "species" or form that the alter takes in headspace, I have very few human alters so this felt important to include.

## Alter Roles
#### Hosts

The overall term of "host" is just an alter who spends the more time fronting than others. Most alters can easily front after discovery, or at the very least share thoughts with the current host, this is most likely due to the religious training we received as a kid. Hosts are usually considered first when it comes to debating waking world looks and dysphorias. There are two kinds of hosts in misk.

host:
This is the alter who fronts the most often of everyone, and takes on most of the day. If they are not actively on console, they are most likely still in [[Front Room]].
<font color="#646a73">Current host is:</font> [[Angel]]

co-host:
The alter that is meant to relieve the host if they get overwhelmed. At this current point in time our host and co-host switch between each other, we have figured out that this allows breaks when needed and also lifts the burden of thinking one will be font-stuck forever. 
<font color="#646a73">Current co-host is:</font> [[Red]]

Inactive Hosts:
Inactive hosts are old hosts or co-hosts who don't front anymore do to past stress. They keep to themselves and stay in misk to take time to heal. This section is mostly to serve as a placeholder as a reminder they exist, and an appreciation for what they went through.
<font color="#646a73">Inactive hosts:</font> [[Akumi]] [[Terror]]

#### Guardians

The main task of a guardian is to prevent or repair damage from the outside world. This can be a range of things but the types that we have a listed below. 

Active:
this type of guardian is aggressive and defensive to prevent perceived threats from the waking world. They come off as spiky or rude if not kept in check. We are currently working to redirect that aggression into something less destructive. The term most know is 'Persecutor' however due to wanting to encourage something more understanding we switched the name.
<font color="#646a73">Current active guardians are:</font> [[Red]] [[Terror]]

Comfort:
It is mostly as it sounds, a comfort guardian is someone who stays mostly in headspace and is around to keep other alters calmer and safer feeling. Some of them try to guide alters through situations, and others just there so that we have someone to lean on.
<font color="#646a73">Current comfort guardians are:</font> [[Pendragon]] [[Unnamed Alters]](49128b<font color="#49128b">♦♦♦</font>)

#### Pain Holders

Pain holders are here to take physical or mental pain. There is only one "role" for pain holder but each alter does it differently.  Some take the mental bearing with something brighter, some take the pain in a way that feels good to them. Others could just be able to handle it better. Pain holders also typically dip into the guardian role as they provide a similar function. To see the difference between each alter their personal pages have how they handle role.

#### Task Holders

This role is for alters who do tasks that they are attuned to, things that as a whole Misk forgets to do or struggles to do. Like pain holders, task holders do things relative to who they are, and what the task is, an alter's personal page will go into what their tasks are.
<font color="#646a73">Current task holders are:</font> [[Trynk]] [[Mesmur]]

#### Misk Keepers

Misk Keepers are alters who mostly stay inside of misk and take care of it. They usually don't front, and when they do its only for a short while. A Misk Keepers main task is to hold memories, these can be things that are too much for the rest of the system to handle, or in general non-important information. If an alter wants access to a memory they have to ask the keeper (there are ways around this, but they are very difficult and it isn't easy to explain with words). Keepers are all at least rulers in Misk, trying to find a memory without guidance from them is like wandering an infinite maze.
<font color="#646a73">Current misk keepers are:</font> [[Silver]] [[Dune]] [[Trynk]]
