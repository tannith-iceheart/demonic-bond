Because we don't think in words alters do not start out with names. Our thoughts are like very vivid imagery, and most of the time uncorrelated imagery. And though our alters have specific identities they "feel like" its not easy to put into words until they are able to become more clear.

Because of this our alters start off as "colors", or more so concepts of colors. In headspace the color can change (and will, depending on the alter) but to keep things organized in the waking world we have all picked a specific color to identify by.
