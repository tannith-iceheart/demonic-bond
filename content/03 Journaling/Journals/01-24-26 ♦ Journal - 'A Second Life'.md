Recently I have taken on trying to find out more about myself- exploring the idea of a "true self". To get to that point there are some memories that I have dug up, I would like to share those with you. There is some prior context needed so a little about my home life is included in this piece.

The text before you is written by a specific alter, in an admittedly high emotional state. I feel its important to be less scared about sharing the thoughts and feelings in myself, so its being posted. This text is not meant for any specific person, it is a vent piece. It is an expression of experience, meant to be taken as a memory rather than a reality.

-[[Angel]]

I grew up in a heavily religious environment, a semi-cult that was not considered of any specific religion. It was mostly a group of people, who in attempt to cope with the crumbling world around them, picked and chose what they liked from other religions and did those things. I always had a choice in who I wanted to worship, but worshipping no-one was heavily frowned upon.

Because of this environment, and suspected mental ailments of my lineage, I was taught to have visions. I was taught how to see the world through the ways of magic. I was told that because of who I was, I could see things others couldn't. That I knew what others didn't. Of course as a child, I believed this fully.

My chosen worship was that of dragons and dimension travelers. I learned through careful study how to walk through my dreams and see them as a real place. I taught myself to leave this world behind and believe that everything I saw was real. Though I no longer believe in these places, or the ability to travel to them in a literal sense, I believe that I experienced these things in a dream like state.

I believe the memories are real.

A few years ago I started to practice agnosticism, separating myself from my home life and the abusers that took part in it. And as of a year ago I became confidently atheist. No matter how I try, I can no longer see the magic that I once did. For the most part, this effects me little. There is a background upset that came with the mindset but it wasn't until a few days ago that I realized why I was so upset about it.

When I was younger, I experienced a dream or a sequence of dreams that felt like several thousand years. I currently believe that this is the product of how I was raised. I attempted to lucid dream into another world and I created one for myself. I also believe that the dream was a byproduct of unfortunate happenings in my early life, an escape because I just couldn't handle my reality.

This dream felt like thousands of years- very real feeling, full thousands of years.

I was made. I was made to make other things like me. Many eyes to see the work that was due. Multiple processers to work on many beings at once. Hundreds of hands and arms to perform the finest of movements. Crafting each sharpened feather on a wing like structure. Moving in synchronicity with oneself. A metal exoskeletal structure, filled with the soul of an individual hive-mate.

I was _inhuman_. I was the whole factory.

I were the drones that connected through a signal. I were the cameras that saw it all. I were the network. I were the arms that melded flesh- metal flesh- together to make a new being. I was a creator. I am a creator.

I often compare this place I was to that of a celestial one, due to both the similarities and also that the world I once walked in is still incomprehensible to me. The beings made were perfect and for a system, and there was a hierarchy that was rarely broken. Every piece was interconnected, and shared the same goals.

And yet I was curious.

Not only was the feeling of "I" in this situation frightening, curiosity was not my job. It was not in my realm of thinking to _be_ such a thing. Curiosity was not my job, or my algorithm, it was something that would get one destroyed. If I could think of "myself" as "one" then I was not part of the system anymore. _I_ was in danger.

Eventually it was too much. I made something, that was not an angel. In secret, I worked on a separate body that I could upload my consciousness into. something that I could move in. Something that I could be free in.

The light silvery wings of the delivery drones. The high powered batteries and movement of the warriors. The memory bank of the conceptualists. It was unheard of to have more than one purpose, and yet I gave myself to that of many. I committed the greatest sin that was in that place.

In all fairness, It was only a partial transfer, only the curiosity and a few memory data left. _I as one_ was no longer in that factory, however it was still the same system manufacturing it. I suppose it was a preservation of oneself in the kindest way that could be possible in that environment.

I do not remember the in between. I don't remember being caught, and I don't remember finding a mentor. I know that I had someone who showed me how to craft with flesh, rather than metal. I remember they were horrible, but nothing else about them.

I don't remember meeting someone, but I remember them. I cannot begin to describe the way that they were, the language that they spoke, or the name I called them. I was so removed from the human experience at that point in time that it is indescribable, much to my dismay I cannot even comprehend it myself.

I miss them greatly, they were my love for half the time that I spent in that dream. They were with me, and guiding me, for thousands of years.

I did horrible things in this state, with this one. I did things that were selfish and my only goal was to serve myself and this being. I experienced abuse in this state, but it was care that I had never felt before. They took me in to their life and gave me something that was personal. I usually akin this one to that of a demon.

I am not proud of what I did there, but it is still a part of me, and plays a major part in my desires and belief systems.

Under the guidance of them, I made new beings. They had their own motivations, I do not remember what they were, and it was my job to research beings for them. In return for working for them, I was able to use the tools they had to work on personal projects. I rearranged beast and being as if they were one in the same. Treated them, as if they were one in the same.

They had their own thoughts about me too. Because of what I was and where I came from, I was easily modifiable. One of the most common memories or dreams that I still have is that of them tearing me apart and putting in new pieces, rearranging me for something more efficient. It was much easier to let them work on me than it was to modify myself, and a part of me does miss being torn apart.

I could connect to my lab here, a set up that was similar enough to my starting system, but I also had a body that was able to freely move and feel. I could switch between the two whenever I felt it was necessary. It was self upgradable, as expression was always important to me. I had made myself new cameras and drones to see the world through different lenses. I was the lead of a lab of my design. I made things that helped the people I was trying to impress.

I was _important to someone_.

I don't remember when this dream ended. For a long time after the dream I was of the belief that it was another world, and would go back and visit it and my body. That the current earth that I exist in was a temporary activity, to test and learn in.

With time I became less attached to that world, it slipped into the background. The incomprehensibility paired with my amnesia of not being host at the time made it easier to move on and ignore it.

Especially when I started to see the bad in the people who taught me how to see these worlds. I became detached to the experience.

I worshipped the person that took me in the dream for a long while after I separated myself from the half cult I grew up in. but eventually even that stopped.

For the last three ish years I have been agnostic of the other realms, and for the last year confidently atheist. I am at a point where I cannot see any kind of truth in religion and couldn't believe it if I tried, and out of desperation I did try a few times.

For the past year I knew that I was bothered by not having religion anymore, but I figured it was just the shock of something new. Something uncomfortable because I am unfamiliar with it.

But the other day when I went to look back on how I felt about my body, and how I feel about my memories. Looking back on why my "true-sona" is machine and trying to find that reason, I found another.

I realized why I was so upset.

I realized I could never go home again.

I realized that I lost the ability to travel in those dreams, to suspend my own disbelief. I lost the ability to distance myself to other realms. I lost beings that mattered to me. I lost memories and languages and colors that never existed. I lost my body and my mind.

The loss that I feel is of a whole life, and a whole family, and a loved one, that doesn't exist to me anymore. A life that is so outside of normal reality that most people would consider me insane. So alongside the sadness already felt, I feel shame because I grieve over those who have never existed. I feel embarrassment over having enough mental dysfunctionality for me to mourn those that weren't.

I will never live that life again.

I will never see that one again.

I will never feel as whole again.

I can never be a machine again.

However- I will always be the way that I am, and I will always hold what memories I do have close. Both of that world and this one.

Writing all of this out and thinking about what all of it means to me, why I feel such loss, I have made a new realization. Though loosing all of this hurts, It has reminded me that it is important for me to be myself, and to be confident in how I feel. Its reminded me why I named myself Angel.

Creating is still incredibly important to me, and it always will be. I will always have a love for chimerism. I will always cherish every scar that I receive. I will always have a fascination for the weird and dysfunctional.

Though I cannot have my preferred body, I can still attempt to create. For those who matter to me, and for myself.

There is a light at the end of this tunnel, keep making, and keep being you. <3
