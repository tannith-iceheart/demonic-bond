Recently I decided to [[Making a Wand • 04-18-26|make a wand]] to try and look into why I was feeling nervous / guilty / embarrassed about it, I have a lot of really (seemingly random) things that I get super nervous about and wands happened to be one of them.

I wasn't particularly interested, but decided to put intention into exploring it anyways. It couldn't hurt to have one / make one. 

Half way through I just- knew- that it had to be [[Sturmfur|Sturmfur's]] colors. I didn't really think about it, just having one suddenly, clicked. 

I still don't know what to do with this, I don't know how or why one would even use a wand. I don't think having one is bad or weird just.. never thought that it would be something in my practice. 

I'm really happy with it though, like genuinely think it turned out super well. I put symbolisms in it that I didn't even realize connected to the dream I had when Sturmfur reached out until after I finished it.

This has been a very interesting experience, everything that has happened has been confirming all of this. I don't know what to think of it because it feels so real, but I am so scared to have it ripped away from me again.
