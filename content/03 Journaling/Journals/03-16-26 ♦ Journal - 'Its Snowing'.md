Today it started snowing. I have been meaning to start practicing my journey for a while and finally gave myself some room to write things down today.

last night storms were incredibly rough. I love storms, they calm me, but even this one woke me up a few times.

Something about the strange weather is bringing hope to me, things will always grow and move and change, even if you don't want them to. 

I could look at this winter storm in spring as a bad omen, but I choose to look at it as something is changing. 

It has been warm for the past few weeks, getting into higher 60s lower 70s. And today, its snowing. 

at 38 degrees outside and it is a blizzard. more snow than I see in the winter. 

I will choose to interpret this as a sign of hope and to move forward

I feel greatly appreciative that I felt the need to open my window earlier even though I don't normally do that

I will choose, to grow and to embrace the unexpected, to carve out the perspective I want to have
