Even before I knew of witchcraft and the significance astrology plays to some, I had been obsessed with my star sign.

I like libra, the balanced nature has always been something that I identify with. I enjoy the symbolism of scales, and lots of the activities I enjoy have to do with balance (mountain biking, dancing, acrobatics, baking).

When I chose my name (I am trans-masc) it was at a time where I voiced little relation to the word. My significant other was very openly aggressive about any superstition, one of the worse treated ones was star signs. I wasn't thinking about the meaning of the name, just that it felt right.

However it still ended up being that said name still has that note of balance to it. "Equinox", when the time of night and day are balanced. I only just realized this as I was researching the Sabbats, I knew the definition before but hadn't linked it to my core yet.

I'm glad to discover this, I think that makes it feel that much more true to me.
