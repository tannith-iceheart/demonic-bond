"There is something in the center"

What's in the middle? I blinked and I'm here now, what is this place, oh. is this everyone?

This feels like everyone, I cant see them though, I can only see red. Why are we always in the same room? We look at each other, we know. Everyone wants what is in the middle.

It kind of doesn't matter what it is, but what it *does* is important. it probably wasn't the same this whole time, or maybe it was, I'm not sure how long he has been in charge of this place.

And I don't mean the castle, I mean of the whole, the island. "Misk". 

Red takes off running, and I follow close after but after he turns the door, he's gone, we have different hallways. Red was right though.. we probably are further back than most, being so close to the door.

If it weren't a race I would want to look around. I like the way he thinks, and his architecture. Its very pretty, and very fitting for him. A light Gothic type castle, silvery décor and trims with patterned grey walls and a sprucy base. All of his things are so detailed.. I want details too...

The thoughts in my head are almost fluid with my own movements down the hall, the more I think and ask the faster I run, the more questions I ask the more turns I take. Wow this place is so pretty.

I don't really think that I want the relic in the center, I just want my questions answered. I think some of the others are like that as well but I'm not sure.. The don't really talk to me much, no one here likes the door. Why am I stuck by it?

*faster. faster. I need to be faster, I have too much to ask.*

I can feel him near, does he want to answer me? Why does he hide things? What am I doing here? can I stop being by the door all the time? can some one visit from time time? Answer me. My thoughts are almost screaming, the halls are twisting.

I come to a cross section with some of the others halls, I see two others here, Azul and Violet. I don't know much about them other than they both only bothered to say hi once. That was at least better than most people on this island.

The thing was.. every one of us was on a different wall, or ceiling depending on who you asked. It didn't strike me until now that sometimes the gravity of the place was changing. per person for that matter.

Does that make it harder? what does that mean? what do they want with this place? what could they want? they already have what they want. They can have anything right? so why care about the center? can you hear me?

After the brief pause, and Violet looking upset, I think she usually looked that way though, we all passed into our respective hallways. There wasn't time to fight between the others in the castle. Flying down the hallways again, and now noticing that sometimes things were upside down, looking for that center.

It was kind of cool to see what it looked like here. Every one of them had their own piece of the island, something that they could form and change however they wanted. I always like seeing the other places that they make. Some are rulers, some renegades, some gods.. I think he considers himself a god?

Not really in the sense that, he thinks he rules over everything but in the sense that.. he makes sure everything plays fair, or his fair. I don't entirely understand, I wasn't told any of this I just kind of assumed it. As you do here because no one *really* talks. we kind of just think at each other..

I think that's what the center does. I think who has it is in "charge" of something. Not sure what, but something important, that could shape a lot of places. That's what I want. Maybe I do want the center? I don't really care about anyone else, or want the responsibility of guarding it because I'm sure it sucks, I just want a piece of land to myself.

Or at least know where my plot is, or know anything about the last time I was here. Would it kill me to remember something from my last visit when I come back here? Navigating would be a lot easier if I wasn't suddenly forgetting everything that I was when I come back to the island.

The door to the hallway is coming closer in, I think they went easier on me *because* I don't remember, and also because I am not after the center piece. But I'm here before anyone else, that's what matters. I'm running out of time.

Silver.

We lock eyes, and he looks at me and smiles almost with pity. Something tells me we have had this conversation before.. Can't I remember? Can you tell me? Can I stay here? Send someone else out the door! please? I can't take it out there anymore. Silver didn't respond, only in the notion that they would let me spend the rest of my stay this time in this room with him. He wont be answering my questions, not today at least.

My thoughts and please come flooding out, almost as if they are drawn out by the relic. Beside silver stands a large sphere, like one of those stands that hold globes, except instead of a globe inside there was a large hourglass. I could tell by looking at it that it wasn't there to count time though, It was holding something else.

It was the only warm tone in the room, a dull bronze and sandy gold inside against all of the grey and silver of the room. Looking around, this was it. *this* was the highest part of Misk, This was the center, the deepest part of the island, and the hardest to reach. 

Thanks for showing me, it does mean a lot.

I couldn't convince Silver in time to let me stay on the island, and asked him to think about it as everything faded out. It grew dark, my body faded, and I existed outside of Misk once again.
