*This dream was written the day after, some details may be lost or misinterpreted.*

**Before Sleeping:**
I took a nap in the evening when I got home because I was so tired and hurt that I couldn't move. I didn't take any medication or consume any particular food or drink. I don't remember How I was laying, but sleep was not easy. 

**The Dream:**
The dream was separated into 3 levels. The first level was a slow, repeated loop, of waking up into a groggy nightmare situation. My body couldn't move but my room looked like my room. Dream logic had me convinced that it was real life, and that I was actually in danger, and every time I realized I was in a dream, I would "wake up" into another

This kind of nightmare is actually where I have the most practice with lucid dreaming. I am particularly fond of when I get them because even though they can be scary, they lead to some of the more vivid and interesting dreams that I have seen.

My usual way of dealing with this when I realize its that kind of dream is to "fall". I will realize I can't move but its a dream, so I "sink" into my surroundings. Several times this has lead me to falling through the floor and into and over other worlds. once I start "falling" I can "fly" in directions to guide myself. Its not easy, as I am still in a groggy state and can't move my body, but it gives more control over the scenario.

This time however I didn't decide to fall, I don't remember how but I managed to get myself up to walk out of the room I was in. I could feel that going through the door was going deeper into the dream.

The second section of the dream was what I call a "shame dream". I don't have them as often anymore but they are a nightmare consisting of social situations that are embarrassing or unpleasant. For example I drive for a living at the moment, so in this shame dream I was constantly wrecking my car into things or other cars, my reaction was delayed.

I don't remember a lot of this section, but I do remember that I eventually got myself injured (intentionally, I've never been injured in a dream before that wasn't my own lucid doing). Injuries, though not painful, usually are enough to disrupt whatever is causing the nightmare parts of the dream.

A few people helped me into an abandoned building and left me there, I wasn't part of the script anymore, they stopped being able to interact with me when in the building and left. The abandoned building had several doors inside, it was like a long hallway.

I walked towards the doors in the hallway, and opened one. When I did, I could feel that this was going even deeper. Something inside me told me that I didn't want to see what was behind it, and I shut the door. But after a moment of consideration I opened the door again. I had this hesitation with all the doors.

When I opened them, inside were "art pieces". It was like you took my memories and made them into a sculpture. After a moment of looking at them, the good or neutral memories turned sour, and cruel. The other side of the moment that I didn't remember.

I only remember the last piece. I walked through the door and my mom and her ex sitting on a couch in the garage they used to have. They were watching something out there as they used to do. It was an actual memory, except when my mom had turned to me, she started to say something horrible. I don't remember what it was exactly, only that it was something sexual in nature.

I got the feeling that I was "supposed" to respond in a way with my head down, as I have all my life. I feel like if I made that decision in that dream I may not have seen what I did, but I talked back to her. I told her off and set a boundary

Her head turned backwards towards me and upside down, her body split into this foul being that turned itself inside out. Blood, bones and muscles all turned around and warping into each other. This moment startled me, but felt real. I knew that I wasn't in danger and I knew that she had no power where I was. She ended up coiling into herself and consuming herself and the environment into nothing.

walking into the last door was a large indoor pool, with large floor to ceiling windows lining the walls. The building was still abandoned, trees and vines claimed the walls and grass the floors. the plant life went from a deep rich green at the floor and continued upwards into a teal. The vivid colors making large contrasts to the grey concrete of the room.

The plants continued upwards into the ceiling that didn't exist, it turned into sky. looking up stars sparked against deep blacks, blues, and purples. It moved and breathed with the room. In the center of the room was a signature of whoever made the art pieces of my memories. In neon like lights a fox like figure, and below signed 'Sturmfur'.

That was when I woke up from the dream, I woke up 3 minutes before my alarm went off. 

**Interpretation**
A few different things come to mind with this. The largest one is that it feels like a sign of progress of me exploring my inner self. What makes me who I am and what I actually enjoy. 

I recently cut off my mom entirely from communication. I have been trans-masc for several years now, and due to a recent argument she started becoming weirdly transphobic towards me. It was just the final thing that happened, she was largely an abusive figure in my life and it took me a long time to realize that. Its just something that really took me by surprise because regardless of the abuse she tended to be pretty LGBT positive. I assume it was for some desperate feeling of control.

Anyways I blocked her recently and I think that specific part of the dream where she turned inside out was a representation of finally seeing the actions she is taking for what they are, rather than denying or being an apologist.

I'm not sure about Sturmfur. The name feels important, especially because I could read it clearly, but I don't know what they represent. I also never saw or heard them, only the signs or displays that they had left.

In general the dream was positive, I am exited to have the skills still to lucid dream after so long of trying not to. I hope that this can be the first step of an actually healthy journey and life. For reference of why I feel this way, look at [[03-16-26 ♦ Journal - 'The Cat Named Hummingbird']]
