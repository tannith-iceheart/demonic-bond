Some notes to keep in mind:
#### Why are some of the notes or journals signed under different names?

Throughout my process I will take personal notes about the things I am writing down. The main reason my book of shadows exists is <i>for me.</i> Sometimes you will see the notes signed off as different names, and that is because I have a disorder called DID. 

Because of this some of my notes may be alter specific, and so they will sign their name. Its very important to us to be open about how this disorder effects us, and this is just another way we do that~

For more information on my alters explore the [[About the System - Road Map]]

#### Why is this every day task / common spell under a different name?
I have a passion for dark or to some "edgy" themes, I adore everything between and under punk / goth / emo umbrella and so my terminology, spell names, and how I present myself follows that desire! I find that It is important to me to connect my work with a style I enjoy so that I can more easily attach to it. 

Also, I can't do anything without making it sound cool. Having a "Fang Sharpening Ritual" is so much more fun to participate in than just regular "brushing your teeth" lol, at least imo
