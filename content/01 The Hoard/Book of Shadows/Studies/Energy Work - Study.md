What is Grounding:

Grounding is a foundation technique to access energy, which is then usually put into spell work or rituals. To ground oneself is to connect with a source of energy, and guide it through themselves. Similar to the way electricity works (<font color="#95b3d7">think of the fences back at your start!</font>)

Types of Grounding:

The kind of grounding I think of first, and the kind I see most often is meditation, specifically visual meditation. Visualizing an image that connects one to the source of energy.

Something that can go with this but not always is music. Something calming or trance-like can be a type of grounding.

After spell grounding; I find this one particularly interesting. Though its intuitive to me to give back extra after use, I never considered it grounding. This kind of thought probably stems from my teachings being to take rather than to share, I will keep this in mind going forward.

For Me:

The kind of grounding that I am going to start with first is going to be a kind of meditation. Sitting low to the floor, feeling my surroundings, and connecting with the air and objects around me. 

Potentially laying out things that I care about around me, as a kid I had a lot of luck with those colorful rocks they sell at tourist traps. I don't currently believe that they actually held the energy that the sellers said, I do believe that they meant a lot to me and so unconsciously I had already been creating a safe space with them.

My intent is to let the care that I put into my environment and items come back to me, and make my home its own circuit over time. This should be helpful in both the witchcraft sense of allowing myself back energy, and also having incentive to take care of the physical.

### Centering

What is Centering:

Centering is being aware and able to read the energy around you, the action of feeling it and guiding it into your center. This is usually used as a part of grounding, but the two aren't mutually exclusive. 

For Me:

I don't have a visual grasp on my energy anymore, if I ever did. I won't neglect past experiences but I also don't take them at full value. 

I have a inclination to center where my lungs are. My breath and air is very important to me, I have spent lots of time singing and training my voice. Remembering to breath is a crucial part for me to work with my energy.
