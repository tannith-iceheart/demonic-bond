##### Tools and Rituals I feel a connection to:
- **Various Divinations**:
	- **Tarot**- I have intention to draw my own tarot eventually, I love cards and have been using a regular playing deck for my tarot for the majority of the time. I have learned a few little card tricks as well, just in general I feel a strong connection to cards / playing cards / suits
	- **Dream Divination**- I love sleeping, one of the things I fell into easier was lucid dreaming. I have always felt a connection to dreams and how I perceive the world. Its one of the bigger reasons I included a [Dreams] section to this grimoire
	- **Smoke Divination** - This was when I was specifically working with dragons. I still feel a connection to them, and the smoke, but haven't reached out yet (as of 03/26) because I am not ready to start worship again. I am sure they will understand my situation when I eventually do.
- **My Voice** - I have trained my voice for years, almost my whole life. Not for witchcraft in particular, I just really really like to sing. I listen to music at least 8hrs a day, and sing for most of that time if others around me aren't sleeping. I use my voice often of spell or energy work.

##### Other misc. things I feel a connection to:

- **Flying**- Flying is so important to me, the sky is so important to me. I love the sky, I want to be in the sky, I miss touching the sky. I often have dreams where I just fly around over cities, plains, forests, anything and everything that I could, and those are my favorite. One of my goals in life is to find ways that I can simulate flying and try them. I love the sky so much, I wish I had the feathers that my spirit form does. Fuck I want to fly so bad
- **Night Time or Dark Iconography**- I love the stars and the sky, The night calms me. I spend a lot of my time working nightshift because I feel more energized at night as well. I have always been fascinated by myths that surround the night, like wherebeast or vampyres. 
- **Shapeshifting**- Though I have grown out of the phase where I thought I could actually eventually shapeshift, I still love the thought. I appriciate metaphors, mythos, and concepts that include a variant of shifting.
- **Pine**- I love pine, I love love any evergreen, but pine is so nice. I grew up around pine and have spent a lot of my childhood climbing and playing around pine. I intend to use pine-sap (for anyone who has ever been around one, you know that isn't difficult to get) for sticky rituals, things that I want to be extended, or that I want to particularly stay around me or my target. I intend to use pine leaves or branches as cleansing tools
- **Water**- I used to swim competitively, grew up around rivers and streams. Often went kayaking or fishing or camping near water, a regular part of my summer routines. I know a lot about rivers and how they work, the power and danger of them, and also the tranquil that they can be. The ever shifting and moving resource.
- **Old Electronics and Handheld Devices**- Probably one of the weirder ones, I really enjoy modding handhelds. As of making this list my current project is making a custom mp3 player for listening to music so that I can rid my monthly subscriptions. I interpret my projects as a kind of art, and the more time I put into them the more energy I do as well. I intend to use things close to me to help raise energy, especially if I use them on a daily basis.
- **The Number 23**- I'm not really sure about this one too much to be honest. I just know that I see it a lot, and that its always been my favorite number. I just felt I should include it in this list.
- **Quarts**- As a kid I lived near enough to a quarts mine that my family went often, thus has ended up with me having tons of quarts. On top of its natural ability to hold energy well, I have a special relationship with it because I have mined these pieces myself, and had them for years. I do intend to go back and get more as a celebration of me starting my journey again!

##### My strongest goals with witchcraft~
I have two main goals that I am focusing on, at least of the beginning of my path.

1. Strengthening my core identity. I struggle a lot with being passive and people pleasing, I want to change that. I want something that can be mine, and I strive to make my life one that is worth living *to me specifically*. I want to show the world that I am not afraid to be myself, and I find the idea that witchcraft is of high interest to me.
2. Finding a community that is willing to accept me. I am aware that not everyone is going to like me, and that is still something I am working on accepting myself. However I would still like to find more company along the way that does enjoy interacting with me. I am exited to learn from others, and hopefully one day teach myself. Freely sharing information is something that is very important to me

##### Core Beliefs and Morals
I have several morals and beliefs that I think I should prioritize in my journey, these are ordered in no particular way as they all mean the most to me.

- **Freedom of Information** - I believe that we should prioritize learning and sharing information to others above monetary gain. I understand there is an amount of information that can be harmful to others if given too quickly. My point is more about disliking those who withhold for selfish gain, rather than withholding out of respect or concern for the other party
- **Creativity should be prioritized** - Being creative is crucial to being. Ultimately, my lifelong goal is to enable others to create as much as possible. This is why when I put out images I don't mind harmless tracing or hard referencing. I don't have any digital property that I wish to keep to myself if it could inspire another.
- **Freedom of Self Expression** - Self expression is something that everyone should have access to, as long as it isn't hurting or stifling others. As someone who is trans, therian, and plural, I see lots of different situations where people judge before they take the time to know. I hope that someday someone can be inspired from me to show who they are to the world as well.
