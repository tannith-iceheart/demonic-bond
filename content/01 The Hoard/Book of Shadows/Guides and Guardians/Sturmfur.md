I am not yet sure who Sturmfur is, where they are from, or how to find that out. I only know that I have been visited by them twice now (maybe more) for guidance.

I did reach out for guidance, so its not out of no where, and it was right as I started getting back into the craft again. They first visited me in [[03-15-26 ♦ Dream - 'Sturmfur'|a dream about my life and troubles.]]

They seem to know a lot about me, and / or have access to a lot about me. I really like them though, I feel a connection to the way that they communicate to me.

What I know so far:
- They are heavy into reclaiming / re-growing and healing. Embracing cracks and imperfections.
- They speak to me through very abstract concepts, dreams being art pieces or just in general not "worded" guidance
- They like symbolism of vines and overgrown places, and the night sky used as paint.
- They seem to be dream based (which is fair, so am I)
- turquoise? (look into what is related to)
- perfect imperfection
- scraps, using what you can, imperfection, cracks, dirt, unevenness
