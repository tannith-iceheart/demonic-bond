Note: this creation is made to be different than normal tarot, I will be learning the regular definitions and names, however I will be substituting some for the sake of connection.

#### Types of Cards Included and Associated Known Names

The Fool -
- A message of new things, the beginning of a journey. The fool is depicted without care, or without knowledge to care. 
	- Upright: beginnings, freedom, innocence, originality, adventure, idealism, spontaneity
	- Reversed: reckless, careless, distracted, naive, foolish, gullible

The Magician - 
- The magician symbolizes the connection between the earth and the astral plains. Associated with reflections of the other plains and access to the four suits. "A full deck"
	- Upright: willpower, desire, resourcefulness, skill, ability, concentration, manifestation
	- Reversed: manipulation, cunning, trickery, wasted talent, illusion, deception

The High Priestess - 
- A mediator between opposing sides; Masculine / Feminine, Good / Evil, Negative / Positive. A guide and believer of any and all.
	- Upright: unconscious, intuition, mystery, spirituality, higher power, inner voice
	- Reversed: repressed intuition, hidden motives, superficiality, confusion, cognitive dissonance

The Empress -
- Associated with Venus, which means that there is complete love, harmony, fertility and luxury by the grace of this goddess. The Empress brings abundance and blessings in the readings of those she meets.
	- Upright: divine feminine, sensuality, fertility, nurturing, creativity, beauty, abundance, nature
	- insecurity, overbearing, negligence, smothering, lack of growth, lack of progress

The Emperor - 
- Associated with the astrological sign of Aries. A ruler figure that is seen as more strict and rough.
	- Upright: stability, structure, protection, authority, control, practicality, focus, discipline
	- Reversed: tyrant, domineering, rigid, stubborn, lack of discipline, recklessness

The Hierophant -
- Associated with Taurus, the definitions I am finding are largely Christian based so this will have to be updated later. Partnered with the High Priestess
	- Upright: tradition, social groups, conventionality, conformity, education, knowledge, beliefs
	- Reversed: rebellion, unconventionality, non-conformity, new methods, ignorance
