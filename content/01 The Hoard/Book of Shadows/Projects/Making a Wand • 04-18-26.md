To be honest, I'm not sure if I'm actually interested in having a wand. There is something about it that makes me nervous, some kind of subconscious judgment. I get a lot of anxiety about it.

But I do want to make *something*. I took some time to think about what (and while a dream catcher and necklace are still on my mind), this felt like something I could do today. At the very least take notes on today.

I also should figure out the root of why I feel the way I do about it, and maybe making one will help me with that.

Notes:
- When researching I saw someone who made a wooden stirring spoon a wand. I find this very interesting and cool.
- I personally would like to carve something
- If I find out I like having a wand, one out of antler would be cool, I like antlers a lot and want to incorporate one into my craft at some point anyways
- (half joke) - tazer as a "wand of protection"
- things to focus on - fire, dragons, carving, confidence, safety
