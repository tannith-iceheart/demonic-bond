*I have been hiking in these woods all my life, and never have I seen anything like that.* staring up at the half-crumbled mansion off the trail. There was an intriguing pull about the place.

Looking for signs of trespassing, there didn't seem to be, I started down the path. The plants and trees seemed to twist inward, the closer to the house the more they changed. Evergreens faded into old dead oaks and peeling birch. 

Though the sun was well in the middle of the sky, the air started to feel like a night-time chill. 

Despite the uncanny feelings around the place, there was still something drawing about it. I step up onto the stone porch, the brick pieces unstable and overtaken by dirt. It almost looked like the building had been plucked from a street and placed in the middle of this forest.
